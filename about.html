Our mission

Many everyday civic problems go unreported because people don't know where to start. CivicPulse gives every resident one place to report an issue, follow its progress and confirm that it was fixed.

We believe a better city is built by many small actions, and that transparency is what keeps those actions going.

What we stand for

Transparency

Every complaint has a visible status, so nothing disappears into a void.

Participation

Citizens aren't just reporters; they verify the outcome too.

Simplicity

Reporting should take a minute, not a visit to an office.

See a problem? Help fix it.

Your report can help make your neighbourhood safer, cleaner and better.
