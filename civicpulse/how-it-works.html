How CivicPulse Works

From a single report to a resolved problem, in four simple steps.

01

Report

Describe the problem, pick a category, add the locality and attach a photo. The more specific you are, the faster the right team can act.

02

Track

Every report receives a unique complaint ID. Enter it on the Track Issue page at any time to see where your complaint stands.

03

Resolve

The responsible team reviews the complaint, moves it to In Progress and marks it Resolved once the work is done.

04

Verify

Citizens confirm whether the fix is satisfactory. If it isn't, the complaint can be reopened.

Frequently Asked Questions

What kinds of issues can I report?

Roads, waste management, streetlights, water supply, drainage and other everyday civic problems in your neighbourhood.

Do I need an account to report?

An account lets you manage your reports in one place, but you can browse issues and track a complaint ID without logging in.

How do I know the status of my complaint?

Use your complaint ID on the Track Issue page. Status is shown as Pending, In Progress or Resolved.

Is this connected to a real municipal system?

No. CivicPulse is a frontend demonstration project, so reports are not sent to any authority.

See a problem? Help fix it.

Your report can help make your neighbourhood safer, cleaner and better.
